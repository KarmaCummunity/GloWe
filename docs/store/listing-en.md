## Full description

Karma Community is where neighbours give, ask and help each other — no money, no middlemen.

**What you can do**
• Give away things you no longer need — furniture, clothes, books, toys, electronics and more
• Ask for something you need and get offers from people nearby
• Share surplus food before it goes to waste
• Volunteer your time or find volunteers
• Offer or find a ride
• Teach and learn — free lessons and know-how
• Donate to verified nonprofits

**How it works**
Post in seconds, with or without a photo. Get matches by city and category, chat, and arrange the hand-off directly.

**A safe community**
Report any post, message or user, and block users. Our moderators review reports and remove objectionable content.

**Privacy**
No ads, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time in Settings.

Join the community — every small act of giving makes a difference.
