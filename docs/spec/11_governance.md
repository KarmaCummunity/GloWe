# 11 — Community governance (D40, D49, D50)

**Vision (D40):** when the community matures, KC publishes a rigid **iron constitution**, with an automatic democratic mechanism on top — and the organization passes fully to the community. The `democracy` domain is its home. GeoPoll (the PM's project) is a possible tool/partner for polls and votes.

## Path (S26)
1. **Founders** — KC management decides; rules transparent in the spec.
2. **Consultation** — non-binding community polls, advisory council.
3. **Binding votes** — in defined areas: new worlds, karma values, community rules.
4. **Handover** — iron constitution in force; automatic mechanism governs.

**Voting right (D49):** one verified person — one vote. A registered org gets a small extra weight as a mark of respect; size is tentative (D49, D55 — not tied to any payment, everything is free for orgs).

**Iron-constitution core: open, to be discussed later (D50).** Discussion starter only (KC fill, not a decision): non-profit; no money between users; privacy & anonymity; free giving; religious and party neutrality; one person one vote; protection of vulnerable populations.

Model: `CommunityPoll`, `GovernanceProposal`, `ConstitutionClause`. Tech gap: G29.
