# 02 — Problem and market

Giving in Israel is rich but scattered. The Registrar of Nonprofits lists ~40,168 active nonprofits; Leket rescues food for ~470,000 people a week via 346 partner nonprofits; JGive connects ~6,969 nonprofits with ~1.78M donors. Still, someone who wants to give away a couch, offer a ride or volunteer an hour goes to a WhatsApp group. The problem is not lack of goodwill — it is **fragmentation, lack of trust and lack of continuity**. (Figures from the PM-side research page, Oct 2026.)

| Pain | Today | What KC does |
|---|---|---|
| Fragmentation | Hundreds of WhatsApp groups, Yad2, Facebook, nonprofit sites | One place with worlds and a feed (D1, D5); import the groups (D38) |
| Trust | No verification, no-shows | Visible verification levels, internal trust engine (D23, D24) |
| No closure | Messages get buried | Closing flows, karma, ratings (D17, D12) |
| Shame to ask | Requests exposed by full name | Full anonymity (D16) |
| Orgs disconnected | Givers don't know where to turn | Org directory in every world/domain (D36, D37) |
| Critical mass | Dedicated ride apps closed (Waze Carpool 2022, Trempy) | Don't start from zero — migrate existing communities (D38, D47) |

**Opportunity.** The largest person-to-person giving markets — rides and item giveaways — already exist and are active, just in a tool not built for them. Whoever gives them a better home wins critical mass.
