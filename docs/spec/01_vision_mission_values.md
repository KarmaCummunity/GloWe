# 01 — Vision, mission, values

**Vision.** A world where every person can give and receive easily, respectfully and safely — and where the community that holds this belongs to its members. Karma Community (KC) is the community layer of giving in Israel, later worldwide: one place where a pot of food, a ride, an hour of volunteering, a couch for a night, a call with a lonely elder or a nonprofit job are all a *post*, and every act of giving is closed, counted and builds trust.

**Mission.** Connect people who want to give with people who need, and both with the organizations already doing the best work in each field (D37). KC does not compete with Leket, Yad Sarah, the SPCA or JGive — it is the community door to them. Long term: an organization that passes fully into the community's hands, under a rigid core constitution with an automatic democratic mechanism (D40, see [11](./11_governance.md)).

## Values
| Value | In practice |
|---|---|
| Giving without return | No price, no profit, no money between users (D4) |
| Dignity & discretion | Anonymity both ways (D16); asking carries no shame |
| Trust without blocking | Verification is shown, not blocking (D23), except sensitive cases (D30, D48) |
| Cooperation | Every nonprofit is a partner (D37) |
| Platform transparency | Open rules, later community decisions (D40); separate from personal privacy |
| Neutrality | No religion and no party politics; spirituality/philosophy is fine (D45, S25) |
| Inclusion & accessibility | Multilingual, accessible; vulnerable populations via professional bodies |
| User control | Sorting, filters, notifications in the user's hands (D26, D27) |

**North star (S32):** successfully closed givings per week.
