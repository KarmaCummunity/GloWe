# 12 — Partnerships (D37)

KC cooperates with every nonprofit body in the world, especially leaders in each field: Leket (food), Yad Sarah and MDA (medical), SPCA (animals), JGive (money), Tamir (environment), ELEM and Lasova (housing), ERAN (elderly), Kol Zchut (knowledge). KC is the community layer that brings people to them, not a competitor.

| Tier (S30) | `PartnershipTier` | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Directory link | `directory` | Appears in the hub (D9) |
| Verified partner | `verified` | Org account, events, jobs, proper-management badge |
| Routing partner | `routing` | KC routes posts to them (event leftovers → Leket, equipment → Yad Sarah) |
| Integration partner | `integration` | System integration, in-org emergency activation (D51) |

Org data source: Registrar of Nonprofits open data (S35). Gap: G30.
