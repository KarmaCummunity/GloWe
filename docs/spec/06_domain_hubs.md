# 06 — Domains and domain hubs (D45)

A domain is an internal tag on every post plus a hub that aggregates it across all giving worlds (S38).

| Domain | slug | Essence | Special rules | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health & medicine | medical | Only verified medical bodies publish (D30) — Yad Sarah, MDA, Ezer Mizion, ADI. Regular users view and contact (S17). The existing philanthropic medical directory (24 bodies, 10 topics) feeds the hub. | No medical advice; blood calls only from medical bodies; local shortage → targeted emergency (D42) | Directory now; feed later |
| Animals | animals | Care & training knowledge, adoption org directory, food donations, feeding/water points (plug-in map). SPCA as lead partner. | No selling animals; adoption via nonprofits | Phase 2 |
| Environment | environment | Plants, seeds, community gardens, recycling; Tamir, Adam Teva V'Din. Cleanup challenges (D43). | Hazardous waste only via licensed body | Phase 2 |
| Elderly & loneliness | elderly | Home visits, phone buddy, errands, tech help, escort to doctor, shared meals, small repairs (S24, D39). | **Home visit requires volunteer ID verification (D48)**; calls/errands do not | Phase 2 (actions already as time posts) |
| Democracy | democracy | Civic rights & duties, elections info, advocacy nonprofits; later polls & votes for KC itself (D40). GeoPoll (PM's project) is a possible partner/tool. | Strict party-political neutrality (S25) | Phase 2 knowledge → 3 polls → vision |
| Philanthropic employment | employment | Huge non-commercial job board for social/nonprofit-sector roles (D41). Any registered org — incl. social ventures and private persons registered as orgs — can post jobs and recruit (D53, D57). | Free posting; social/philanthropic roles only; no karma on paid jobs | Phase 2 |
| Sport | sport | Equipment, free coaching, community sport groups; tied to challenges (D43, D44). | No commercial competitive sport | Phase 2 |
| Science & technology | tech | Tech volunteering for nonprofits, mentoring, donated devices, open source (D44). | Devices handed over only after personal-data wipe | Phase 2 |
| History & heritage | heritage | Vision: a huge public project mapping history on an accessible, gamified, visual timeline (plug-in) (D44). | A source per item; no divisive content | Phase 3 / vision |
| Culture & art | culture | Instruments (items), tickets/classes (experiences), community art (time/events). | Free only | Phase 2 |
| Spirituality & philosophy | spirituality | Discussion circles, meditation, philosophy study groups, books. Institutional religion excluded (D45). | No missionary content or solicitation | Phase 2 |
